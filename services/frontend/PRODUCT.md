# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Solo and small-business marketers — a founder, owner, or one-person marketing function without an in-house agency — who need a complete ad campaign built quickly from a brief, without hiring out strategy, copywriting, design, voiceover, and video production separately.

## Product Purpose

Turns a single campaign brief (business details, goal, tone, target platforms, optional voice/audience notes) into a full multi-asset marketing campaign — strategy, social copy, storyboard, images, voiceover, and final video — generated by an AI pipeline, with a human-approval step before anything is treated as final. Success is a coherent, ready-to-publish campaign produced from one brief with minimal manual assembly.

## Positioning

The mechanism is coherence plus a review gate, not any single generated asset being best-in-class: all six outputs are generated from and stay consistent with one strategy/brief, and nothing is final until a human explicitly approves it (`AWAITING_APPROVAL`/revision workflow already implemented in the worker pipeline). A neighboring product assembled from separate point tools (a chat model for copy, a design tool for images, a separate video editor) could not truthfully make either claim — the assets wouldn't share one strategy, and there'd be no built-in checkpoint before publishing.

## Operating Context

The core workflow: fill out the campaign brief form (`/campaigns/new`) → submit (idempotent creation via an `Idempotency-Key` header, enforced today) → the backend workflow generates strategy → copy → storyboard → images → video (voiceover generation happens as part of this pipeline) → the campaign reaches a review/approval state → a human approves or requests revisions → the campaign is finalized. The frontend today covers brief submission; the monitoring/review surface (`/campaigns/new/:campaignId` detail page) is being built out as part of this pass, currently backed by fixture data rather than live polling.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Campaign creation validates business name, product/service, business description, campaign goal, at least one platform, tone, and language as required; target audience, key message, call to action, and brand colors are optional.
- Platform options today: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn.
- Campaign creation is idempotent — retried submissions with the same idempotency key do not create duplicates.
- The backend models a granular campaign status (`CREATED → QUEUED → GENERATING_STRATEGY → GENERATING_COPY → GENERATING_STORYBOARD → GENERATING_IMAGES → RENDERING_VIDEO → READY_FOR_REVIEW → REVISION_REQUESTED/APPROVED/FINAL`, plus `FAILED`/`CANCELLED`), but the frontend's Campaign Detail page in this pass uses a simplified three-state model (pending/generating/ready) per asset, driven by fixture props rather than live data — real polling against `useCampaignDetail` is a known follow-up, not done here.
- Undecided: multi-user accounts, team/agency workspaces, and billing/pricing do not exist yet and are out of scope until a real need is confirmed.

## Brand Commitments

None yet — no logo, formal brand guideline, or named identity beyond the product name "AI Marketing Campaign Agent" used in the current UI. The visual language established for `/campaigns/new` (blue/indigo primary, teal/emerald/cyan/coral/amber accents, Space Grotesk display + Inter body type, restrained motion) is the incumbent system this pass extends — not yet a formally committed brand identity.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real customers, testimonials, case studies, press mentions, or pricing exist. Do not fabricate any of this on Home or Campaign Detail.

## Product Principles

1. Coherence over point-solution quality — every generated asset should read as part of one campaign, not six disconnected outputs.
2. Nothing is final without human approval — the review/approval gate is a product commitment, not an incidental workflow step.
3. Show real state honestly — generation progress and campaign status should never be faked or implied before it's true (carried into the frontend as "no fake progress bars, no decorative loaders").
4. Low-friction for a solo operator — one brief, minimal manual assembly, no requirement to coordinate multiple tools or people to get a campaign out.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement beyond standard web accessibility (keyboard operability, visible focus states, sufficient contrast) — already a working constraint on `/campaigns/new` and carried forward to every surface in this pass.
